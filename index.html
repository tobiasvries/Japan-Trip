Day 2 · Friday, 8 May 2026
Cinque Terre 🎂
Gabi's Birthday — La Spezia & the Villages
🎂 Gabi's Birthday 🚗 ~1h20 each way 🏖 Monterosso
07:30
Depart
Drive to La Spezia — ~1h20 via A12
⌄

Take the A12 motorway towards La Spezia. Park at Europa Park underground car park, Piazza Europa — around €15/day.

⚠Never drive into the Cinque Terre villages themselves. Cars are banned and fines are severe. La Spezia is your base.
ℹBuy the Cinque Terre Card (~€18) at La Spezia station — gives unlimited train rides between all 5 villages plus trail access.
📍 Europa Park Car Park
09:30
Beach
Monterosso al Mare 🏖
⌄

The only Cinque Terre village with a proper sandy beach. Head to Spiaggia Fegina in the new town. Choose between the free section or a paid lido (€30 umbrella + 2 sunbeds). Sea is around 17–18°C in May — refreshing.

⭐Best CT village for a proper morning beach session. The old town is also gorgeous for a morning stroll.
📍 Monterosso al Mare
12:30
🎂 Birthday Lunch
Ristorante Miky — Birthday Celebration
⌄

Michelin-recognised Ristorante Miky in Monterosso. Order the trofie al pesto and the fritto misto di mare. This is the main birthday meal.

⚠Must reserve ahead — this is the best restaurant in CT and fills fast, especially on weekends.
⭐Tell them it's Gabi's birthday when you book AND when you arrive. Expect something special from the kitchen.
📍 Ristorante Miky
14:30
Village
Manarola — the most photogenic village
⌄

Train from Monterosso to Manarola. The coloured cliffside houses stacked above the sea are CT's most iconic view. Rock swimming at the marina if you fancy a dip. Walk up to Punta Bonfiglio viewpoint for the classic shot.

📍 Manarola 📍 Punta Bonfiglio
17:00
✨ Golden Hour
Nessun Dorma Aperitivo Bar
⌄

The terrace bar perched above Manarola with arguably the best view in all of Italy. Order a Spritz or the local Sciacchetrà dessert wine. Time the golden hour light perfectly.

⚠Arrive early — the terrace fills up well before sunset. Go at 17:00 to get a prime spot.
⭐This is the moment of the trip. Sciacchetrà is a sweet amber wine made only in CT — worth every cent.
📍 Nessun Dorma
19:00
Return
Train to La Spezia → drive home
⌄

Train back to La Spezia, collect the car from Europa Park, and drive home ~1h20 on A12. Home around 21:30.

Day 3 · Saturday, 9 May 2026
Pisa & Lucca
Tower, gelato, and cycling the medieval walls
🗼 Pisa 🚲 Lucca walls 🍦 Best gelato
08:30
Monument
Piazza dei Miracoli & Leaning Tower 🗼
⌄

Arrive before 10am to beat the tour bus crowds. The "Field of Miracles" with the Tower, Baptistery, and Camposanto is genuinely stunning at this hour. Get the combo ticket for all three.

⚠Tower tickets sell out fast, especially on Saturdays. Pre-book at opapisa.it before leaving home — Saturday slots can go weeks ahead.
📍 Piazza dei Miracoli 🎟 Book Tower
10:30
Gelato
Arno walk + La Bottega del Gelato 🍦
⌄

Stroll down to Piazza Garibaldi along the Arno. La Bottega del Gelato is right on the piazza — the pistachio is extraordinary. Don't skip it.

⭐Widely considered the best gelato in Pisa. Pistachio cone is non-negotiable.
📍 La Bottega del Gelato
11:30
Outdoor
Lucca — Cycle the Walls 🚲
⌄

Drive ~30 min from Pisa to Lucca. Park free at Parcheggio Palatucci just outside centro (Lucca is ZTL — camera-enforced). Rent bikes and cycle the famous 4km tree-lined loop on top of the intact Renaissance walls. Views of rooftops, bell towers, and the Apuan Alps.

⚠Lucca centro is a ZTL zone. Do NOT drive in — park at Palatucci outside the walls.
📍 Parcheggio Palatucci
13:00
Lunch
Piazza dell'Anfiteatro — lunch & explore
⌄

One of Italy's most unusual squares — an oval piazza built on the outline of an ancient Roman amphitheatre. Pick any of the trattorias around it, all reliable. After lunch: climb Torre Guinigi (trees growing from the top!) and browse the elegant Via Fillungo for shopping.

📍 Piazza dell'Anfiteatro 📍 Torre Guinigi
16:00
Return
Drive home ~60 min
⌄

Back to La Locanda di Corrado. Dinner locally this evening — Osteria La Saletta, Zafferano, or hotel dinner.

Museum
Uffizi Galleries 🖼
⌄

Skip-the-line tickets essential — book well in advance online. Allow 2–3 hours. Botticelli's Birth of Venus and Primavera, Caravaggio, Raphael, Michelangelo. One of the greatest collections in the world.

⚠Uffizi is CLOSED on Mondays. This day must be Sunday, May 11 — do not swap with Day 5.
📍 Uffizi Gallery 🎟 Book Tickets

Landmark
Ponte Vecchio & Piazza della Signoria
⌄

Just 2 minutes from the Uffizi. The medieval jewellers' bridge is endlessly photogenic — best in the early morning light. Then the Piazza della Signoria: free open-air sculptures including Cellini's Perseus and Giambologna's Rape of the Sabines.

📍 Ponte Vecchio 📍 Piazza della Signoria
12:00
⭐ Highlight
Pasta Class Florence 🍝 — ~3 hours
⌄

5.0 stars, 2,269+ reviews — one of the most loved experiences in Florence. Make three types of fresh pasta, drink wine throughout, then sit down and eat everything you made. Recipes emailed after.

⚠Book as soon as possible — slots fill weeks ahead and this is the most booked-out class in Florence.
⭐The single best activity of the trip. Don't miss it.

Oltrarno or Mercato Centrale
⌄

Option B
Mercato Centrale
Florence's covered food market. Downstairs is fresh produce; upstairs is a food hall with pasta, truffles, wine bars. Great afternoon browse.

Essential Tips

📅
Public Holidays
No Italian public holidays May 7–12, 2026. All sites open normally.
🖼
Uffizi
Closed Mondays. Sunday May 11 only. Book skip-the-line well ahead.
🛍
Barberino Outlet
Closed Mondays in May. Best: Sunday May 11 til 21:00. Free parking.
🛍
The Mall
Open Mon–Sat 10–19. Gucci, Prada, Valentino. ~50 min via A1.
🚆
Cinque Terre
Park La Spezia ~€15/day. CT Card ~€18 = unlimited trains + trails.
🚗
ZTL Zones
Florence, Pisa & Lucca are camera-enforced. Park outside, walk in. Fines €100+.
☀️
May Weather
18–24°C, mostly sunny. Sea ~17–18°C. Sunscreen + light layer for evenings.
💳
Cash
Many trattorias prefer cash. Keep €30–50 on hand. "Coperto" (€2–4) is a legal cover charge.
🚗
Car Tips
SGC FI-PI-LI is toll-free. A1/A11 have tolls — keep change or card. Fill up at Autogrill stations, not small village stations (more expensive).
🍷
Ordering Wine
Always ask for house Chianti (vino della casa) — cheaper and often better. "Un quarto di rosso" = 250ml carafe.
📱
Data & Maps
EU roaming from NL works fine. Download offline Google Maps for Stabbia area before leaving.
🎂
Birthday Tips
Tell every restaurant it's Gabi's birthday. Italians absolutely love this — expect a free dessert, a candle, or the whole room singing "Auguri!" It genuinely happens.
